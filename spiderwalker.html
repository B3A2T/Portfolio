ROBOTICS PROJECT

SpiderWalker

Designing a multi-legged walking robot using Fusion 360, servo motors, and mechanical engineering principles.

← Back to Robotics Lab

Project Overview

SpiderWalker is a robotics project focused on designing and developing a multi-legged walking platform. The current prototype uses servo motors and custom-designed mechanical components modeled in Fusion 360. The long-term goal is to develop a larger walking platform capable of supporting a person.

Mechanical Design

Leg Link Prototype

The SpiderWalker leg-link prototype was designed in Fusion 360. The component connects to a DS3218 servo horn and forms part of the robot's leg mechanism.

The design was revised to correct alignment issues and eliminate interference between components.

Engineering Challenges

Servo and Leg Link Alignment

Problem

The original leg-link design was misaligned with the servo horn, causing components to intersect.

Solution

The leg-link design was revised in Fusion 360. Component positioning and joint relationships were adjusted to eliminate interference.

Result

Fusion 360 interference testing detected no remaining collisions, and the assembly successfully demonstrated rotational movement.
